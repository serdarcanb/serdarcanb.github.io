About

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineer. I started with bare-metal servers, Proxmox, VMware and KVM virtualization and networking at a telecom operator. After that I worked on Kubernetes, CI/CD, GitOps and monitoring at BİTES, Insider, arabam.com and ICRYPEX, across AWS, Google Cloud and Huawei Cloud.

Today I work at LimonCloud, a cloud consultancy and managed services provider. My days are production support for 20 active customer accounts: incident root-cause work, EKS upgrades, an Azure AKS to EKS migration, cost and security reviews, and backup standards.

I also build internal tools in Python. Two of them are a read-only AWS inventory with 85 scanners and an AWS architecture and pricing web app with its own MCP server.
